Pirate Gold

Propose the greediest split Pirate A can pass.

0:00

Rules of the chest

  1. Pirates A through E find 100 gold coins. A is most senior, E most junior.
  2. The most senior pirate alive proposes a split of every coin. All living pirates vote, including the proposer.
  3. The proposal passes if at least half vote yes. In a tie, the proposer has the casting vote — exactly half is enough.
  4. If it fails, the proposer is thrown overboard and the next senior proposes.

Every pirate’s goal, in order:

1 Survive 2 Maximize coins 3 Fewer pirates left alive
The question

What is the maximum number of gold coins Pirate A can keep while ensuring the proposal is accepted?

The vote holds

A keeps the greediest share that still buys a passing vote.
