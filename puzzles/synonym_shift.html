🔀 Synonym Shift

Move a single letter from one word to the other to make a pair of synonyms.
0:00
How to play: Click/tap and drag a letter from one word, and hover it over the other word. The adjacent letters will slide out of the way to show where it will land. Release the letter to make the shift!
Example: In “Boast - Hip,” drag the “s” from Boast and insert it inside Hip to create “Boat - Ship.”
